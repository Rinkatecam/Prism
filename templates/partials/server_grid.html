So the hue is kept and the ink is nudged toward black/white in 6% steps, capped at 24, until the composite clears 4.5:1 in EACH theme — matching the JS solver in servers.html and the identical macro pair in server_card.html byte-for-byte, and verified to agree with tools/tag_ink.py by tests/test_tag_ink.py.
